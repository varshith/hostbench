# Advertise

HostBench readers are developers deciding where to host a site. Many arrive from searches like "cloudflare pages vs vercel" or from the monthly report, and they are looking at latency and uptime data when they see your placement.

We sell two kinds of placement. Both are labelled, and neither changes the data.

## Featured Provider

A listing for a hosting platform, shown beside the leaderboard and on relevant host and comparison pages. [VERIFY: placement locations] It is labelled "Featured".

You get:

- your logo, name and a one-line description
- a link to a page of your choice
- a place beside the measurement data, visible to readers comparing hosts

If we already measure your platform, your listing links to your host page. If we do not, we can add you to the measurement pipeline on the same terms as every other host. Your results are then published whatever they show.

Price: [PRICE_FEATURED] per month.

## Sponsor

A sponsor slot shown beside the data across the site. [VERIFY: placement locations, and whether the monthly report carries it] It is labelled "Sponsored" and suits products that are not hosts.

You get:

- your logo and a short line of copy
- a link to a page of your choice

Price: [PRICE_SPONSOR] per month.

## What you do not get

Paying for a placement buys space on the page. It does not buy:

- any change to how your platform, or a competitor's, is measured
- a position in the ranked table, which is sorted by measured results only
- removal or delay of unfavourable results
- early access to data or editorial review of the monthly report

Featured and sponsored placements always sit outside the ranked table. We will decline any arrangement that asks for otherwise.

## Who should advertise

The best fit is a mid-sized hosting platform that performs well but gets compared less often than the largest names. A place beside the numbers puts you in front of readers already comparing. Infrastructure tools that sit next to hosting also fit: uptime monitoring, CDNs, DNS providers and observability services.
